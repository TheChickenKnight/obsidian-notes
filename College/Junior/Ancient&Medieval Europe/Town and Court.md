# Medieval Towns
Either developed around castles (**burghs**) or cathedrals, or could be "chartered cities" organized by the lord to make money.

- The oldest and largest cities were concentrated in the Italian peninsula.
## Medieval Trade
Many of these cities trade goods with each other. this creates the first instance of demand outstripping supply and international trade.

## Organization
- Initially led by a nobleman or bishop
	- lots of taxes
- This was until wealthy merchants worked together to object taxes
This led to
### Guarantees of Freedoms
- To hold property
- To move freely
- To manage themselves
- To not be taxed to death
Now that these towns focus on making money, nobles could instead leave these towns mostly alone and they would make a lot more money.
## Town Life
The people consisted of:
- Wealthier peasants
- Runaway serfs
- Younger sons of nobility(not firstborns)
- Artisans, tradespeople
Not just people of European descent

### Jewish History

Jewish people were also in these cities, forced to wear distinctive clothing and live in separate sections of the city. 

#### Usury
Lending money at interest was forbidden to Christians, so Jewish communities often did banking instead.

### Craft Guilds
Organizers of workers in towns (Specifically one trade)
- ex: cobblers, saddlers, weavers, tailors, smiths, bakers, etc.

Craftsmen were limited to practicing their own craft.

Trade guilds decide when people become what category
- Apprentice < Journeyman < Master Craftsman
- Master Craftsmen would take on apprentices for on-the-job training.

#### Female Guilds
Unfortunately, freedom and opportunity wasn't freely given to all.
Some trades were dominated by women:
- spinning
- weaving
- brewing
- baking
Some uncommon Female guilds existed with the roles
- Apprentice < Journeywoman < Mistress

Usually governed by men. 
Their labor was essential but undervalued and even erased from records.

## Feudal Culture
### Chivalrous Code
To be courageous, loyal, true to one's word, and protect the weak.

### Troubadours
Poets of the high middle ages who would write "love letters" to women.
#### Courtly Love/Ideal
Created a fantasy sort of of romance, separating the original realistic love to stylized, idealized love.
### Tournaments
Pitched battles where knights would face each other in blunted weapon combat.

## The Rise of University
**circa 1100-1200 C.E.**
Europe's need for educated administrators led to **cathedral schools evolving into universities**
- Leads to more social mobility (not for women or jewish people)
### The Seven Liberal Arts
Advanced study that formed the core curriculum in theology, medicine, or law.
