# Muhammad
**circa 571-632 C.E.**
- pre-Islamic Arabia (present day Saudi Arabia).
	- between Byzantines and Persians
	- Inhabited by:
		- Farmers
		- Bedouins
- Unlike the [[Byzantine Empire]], we have an exact start date of the Islamic Empire because we know Muhammad started it.
- From a Quraish family, Orphaned early in life but became a trader. 
	- He was born into the dominant aristocratic tribe.
## Mecca
- Muhammad was a successful merchant from **Mecca**
	- junction of major trade routes
	- Polytheistic religious center
		- **Kabah**, contained a special sacred rock.
	- War between the Byzantines and Persians allowed for increased trade
## Religious Experience
In his late 30s, he gets a vision of the Angel Gabriel commanding him to recite God's word.
- This became the **Qu'ran**.
	- Made him a monotheist.
		- One God, **Allah**, and **Muhammad** is His Prophet.
	- The people of **Mecca** felt threatened by him, because he was basically making their **Kabah** useless
- Stated he received a clear message than Moses and Jesus
## Hijra
**circa 622 CE**
The migration from **Mecca** to **Medina**
- Begins the Islamic calendar
### Hadith
stories and sayings of Muhammad
### Umma
The combined community of Muslims.

alms

# Sunni/Shi'a
Succession Conflict after Death of Muhammad. 
Unfortunately he didn't say what should happen after.
## Abu Bakr
**632-634**
Muhammad's close companion and father-in-law. Died two years later.
## Umar
**634-6448**
Military Admin Leader. 
Was Assassinated.
## Uthman
**644-656**
Twice Muhammad's son-in-law; the great grandson of the **Umayyad** clan. 
Was Assassinated.
## Ali 
**656-661**
Muhammad's cousin and son-in-law
Contested leadership -> Split begins.
Was Assassinated.
## The Split
A split that still exists today.
### Sunni
Caliph chosen by the community (majority)
### Shi'ite
Caliph should come from Muhammad's family, starting with Ali (minority at the time).
# How did Islam Spread?
- Expansion began under the first caliphs, after Muhammad's death.
- Rapid conquests spread out of Arabia into
	- the Levant
	- Egypt
	- North Africa
- By the early 700s, armies crossed into Spain under the **Umayyads**
## Military Success
Expansion built a vast empire. conquering many people and territories.
## Political Opportunity
**602-628 C.E.**
Timing mattered.
Byzantine and Sassanian empires had weakened each other in long words
## Relatively Tolerant Policies
Conquered Christians and Jews (People of the Book), were not forced to convert, protect as **dhimmis** if they paid a tax. Some minority groups (Copts, Nestorians, Jews) may have welcomed Muslim rule after persecution under Byzantines.

# The Umayyads
First hereditary dynasty of caliphs; **Sunni "successors of Muhammad".
- Capital moved to **Damascus**
	- closer to Byzantines
	- further from holy sites
# Abbasid Caliphs
**750-1258 C.E.**
They dethrone and virtually eliminate the Umayyad Dynasty, except for one prince who escapes to Spain.
