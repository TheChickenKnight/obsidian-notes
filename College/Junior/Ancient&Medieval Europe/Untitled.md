# Architecture
## The Romanesque
**11th Century**
- Thick stone walls, small windows $\rightarrow$ dim interiors
- Rounded arches, barrel vaults
- Massive piers and towers
- Symbolism: fortress-like faith and endurance
- Pilgrimage churches along major routes
## The Gothic
**12th-13th century**
- Pointed arches, ribbed vaulting
- Flying buttresses support thin walls
- Expansive stained-glass windows
- Emphasis on height, light, transcendence
- Symbolism: faith aspiring upward.
