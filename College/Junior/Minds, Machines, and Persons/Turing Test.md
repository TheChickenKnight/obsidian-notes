# Automaton
*Calculator*
Simply calculating what instructions it's given.


Since the beginning of AI, the issue has been mirroring the things we do with our own mind.
*How can we understand someone is Intelligent?*
*Is problem solving this metric?*

## Computational Theory Level -> Problem Solving
The ability to recognize a presented solution. To re-frame the problem, and to refute/modify knowledge given. The ability to interact with it's environment.
### Functionalism
The idea that the human brain and machine are capable of doing the same thing, even though of their physical traits. Different models, but able to generate intelligent activity through the manipulation of symbols. The only thing changes is what they're made of.

### Criteria
#### Real-time
If it is able to operate in real time.
#### Adaptive behavior
Must be able to adapt to its environment
#### Language
Must have a language that others understand
#### Self-aware
Must be in the same way humans are at least.
#### Evolution
Must be not stagnant. This is over time. 
#### Efficient
Must be able to try to find the most efficient strategy.
