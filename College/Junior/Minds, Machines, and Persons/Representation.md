# Analogical
To represent via similar looking things. ex. maps.
## Mental Imagery
the power humans have to accurately represent reality.
## Kosslyn
Psychologist/Philosopher

Writes a book with Ball and Reiser

Does an experiment by giving many people a map, taking it away, and watching them redraw it. This allowed them to see that it takes people the same amount of time to look at a map from one point to another to imagine it in their mind, making them believe that Mental Imagery is one-to-one.
# Procedural
To represent via a sequence of steps, list, or actions. ex. recipes

## Procedural Semantic
The understanding of a word or a sentence consists of a sequence of orders. Words point to the procedure needed in order to imagine the sentence.

> There is no need for us to need an image of symbolic state.

This means whenever we understand something, its more that we understand how, not what. How to do something vs what it is.
