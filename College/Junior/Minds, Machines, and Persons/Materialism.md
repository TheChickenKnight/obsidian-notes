## Reductionism/ Identity Theory
*Introduced in the 1950s*
The theory that we can reduce things like the mind to its physical states.
> If there is no brain, then there is no mind.

Any mental state is nothing but a brain state. Any mental state can be reduced to a certain brain state.

### Putnam
A dis-creditor of this theory.

> the fact that mental states has to be identical to brain states implies that when two people think the same thought, their brains are configured the same. This is strange though, because some people can think of the same thing but have different reactions to it.
