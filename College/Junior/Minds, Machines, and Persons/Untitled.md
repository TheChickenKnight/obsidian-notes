# K. Barad

## Eyes of the Observer

### Von Foester
Philosopher and Physicist.
> Whenever we are distinguishing things, whenever we are assigning an **existential domain** to something, WE are doing it. There is no difference. We wouldn't be able to distinguish this without deciding ourselves.

#### Existential Domain
The space where the distinction of an entity makes sense withing the observer's experience and action.

### Causation
There has to be a cause to distinguish a thing.
### Deduction
This thing has to be deduced from another thing.
