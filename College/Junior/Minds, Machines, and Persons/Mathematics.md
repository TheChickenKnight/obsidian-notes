# Is Mathematics objective or subjective?
Mathematics is clearly objective from my subjective viewpoint. At least, if it's 100% correct. The issue is that we as humans can never be fully sure if what we have devised will always work. But, Mathematics, in it's purest form, is the objective rules and laws that line up with each other. There is no way that math should differ from person to person, as it makes up the universe uniformly. 
# Is it Man-made, or inscribed somewhere, somehow in the universe?
Mathematics are just the rules, the patterns of the universe. It's not man-made, but simply discovered by man. The universe's origins are obviously obscure, but however it was made, Mathematics comes into play. The universe may seem random, but in reality it is just chaotic. For some reason, the universe has rules and it follows them. Us as humans have just begun to see these patterns and label them ourselves. To create math would be to invent gravity, but even before humans, we can assume gravity still behaved the same way. In line with numbers.
# What are numbers?
Numbers are not physical, obviously. As humans, we tend to group objects together (Or maybe the universe tends to do so). To associate a group, there are distinct objects within it, which obviously differ from each other. To categorize each one locally, the concept of counting was designed. Numbers are a construct, a prefab of sorts meant to be placed on objects in order to give them some value, order, or something else of that sort. In this way, numbers can be considered invented by man, but even still the concept of multiple objects, or a scale in which a value can range from, comes from the inherent law-abiding nature of the Universe.

In extension, everything you do and say is governed by math.

1. Investigate fundamental laws of the mind's reason.
2. Expressing those laws in symbolic language 
3. Giving a mathematical foundation to logic

He associated thinking with computation.
