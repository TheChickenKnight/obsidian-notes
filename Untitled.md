# Ship

## Silgichor (Me)
Navigator
Sea Elf
Silgichor was born to the Amarathiné of the southeastern ocean, a small collection of passive sea elves. Although not as strong, Nature's Vengeance still affected their underwater home. Around 100 ANV,  the wandering wizard Vaelion Ardros left the safety of the Bubbles to establish his own. Instead of creating the normal land settlement, on his adventures, he came across Amarathiné during a ship accident. He fell in love with Silgichor’s ancestor Liraethynn, and together figured out how to weave the Tideward Sphere: an adaptive protective Bubble that works underwater. Three generations later, Silgichor spent her youth learning the methods given by Vaelion to become a wizard and support the dome, but she despised the suffocating stillness of maintaining the Sphere and longed for the sky she had never seen. When the pressure of legacy became unbearable, she fled during a moment when her mother was concentrating on the Sphere. 
She swam up until she collapsed at the ocean's surface, floating around until she was "rescued" by Cinnevérre (captain), Kant(Bodyguard), and Van Darranto(Fisherman/cook) aboard the small storm-chasing sloop _Nature’s Wake_. They are a crew of researchers more than adventurers, a small group with dreams to mitigate Nature's Vengeance's destruction. The crew took her in cautiously at first, but after finding out on her innocent nature, welcome her in as their much needed navigator. For they are heavily understaffed, being on the sea for their first week and severely underestimating the dangers. In a couple more days, the under-prepared crew start to sail in the direction of a tempest, until a pirate attack from a ship, *The Liberator* shattered the peace, tearing their mast, killing those she trusted, and the larger ship's navigator. 
At first it seemed she would become a slave, but Vadoliir changed that. The dwarf captain of  *The Liberator* was deeply against slavery for some reason, confusing his crew and causing doubts in his leadership. In two weeks, during a passing of Nature's Vengeance, a mutiny broke out, only for the catastrophic tempest to wreck the ship and hurled them into the raging waters. Memories flash in Silgikore's mind as she remembers the fate of her previous crew. For hours it seems, she searches the well spread out wreck and finally finds a shaken Vadoliir, extremely relieved to not be left alone, not noticing that he is any different. Vadoliir clearly wanted to head to land, but in his exhausted state, he needed sustained help from the sea elf in order to slowly make progress back to the coast. In this extended period of treading water, Vadoliir told Silgichor much about his own history. Of his deceased family, his times as a slave, and how he then became a pirate who avoids the law. Silgichor did her best to follow along, and eventually they made it to Erast's coasts. They spend some time at an inn to rejuvenate, staying out of sight of the guards, for the most part. One particular event followed that guards came up to her and proceeded to question if she had seen the man in a held up picture. There was no doubt in her mind that this was supposed to be a representation of her only friend, but from what she remembers, this is how they could be split apart. She thinks back to what was said about Vadoliir skirting the law, and decides to say no. This must be the moment where to lie. Not knowing if she did the right thing or not, once the guards left, she was afraid of what the consequences would become, but it turns out the relieved Vadoliir who came up from behind a barrel wanted this outcome in the first place. 
A week in Erast goes by, and the Convoy takes place. This is a very non-frequent group of many travelers who go from Bubbled city to city in order to safely cross the lands, with the aid of guards. According to Vadoliir, this is the best direction for them to travel in. However, Silgichor was still slightly unsure. This confusing place gave her two choices: Go back to Amarathiné, or stay with Vadoliir, straying further from the ocean than ever before. In the end, she didn't want to give up on her only genuine relationship from her perspective, and together they went on the Convoy. Silgichor learned of many different concepts, ideas, animals, places, and even weather patterns she had never heard of before. Of course, not a lot of it stuck. This journey took course over many months, and grew closer to Vadoliir.
