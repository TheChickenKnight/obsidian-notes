### The Peasant Conscript



Born in the past, archaic town of Dregsburg, Haniel’s early life was entirely devoid of interaction with anyone but her family. They grew up in a quiet, muddy hovel where the most advanced technology was a blacksmith's bellows and a wooden crossbow. Neglected and abused by their parents, they spent all of her time starving and talking to the shadows dancing in the walls of their dark room. Eventually, their emotions manifest within the shadows, creating their first friend. Their parents after finding out of their creation, became afraid and began to hide the child and their dog even more. Unfortunately, the truth always comes out. 

In 122 ZCE, a battalion of Liquidators encroached on the outskirts of Dregsburg, viewing the town harboring an anomalous dog as a blight that needed purging. Panicking, the town hastily threw together a defensive militia. Haniel's parents and others were forcefully drafted to hold a scavenged trench line outside the town. Facing a severe lack of manpower, their parents even dragged out Haniel, thinking that they could finally get a use our of them.

When the Liquidators attacked, Haniel was thrust into a mechanized hell they couldn't comprehend. They were deafened by the roar of artillery and blinded by chemical smoke. Shrapnel rained down on their position, mortally wounding Haniel in the chest, but the frantic, panicked screaming of their parents kept them from making a sound. 

Just as the Liquidators breached the wire and prepared to burn the trench to ash, the air pressure dropped. Reality bent, and the Unifiers teleported directly into the center of the fray. 

The battlefield fell dead silent before the slaughter began. The Knight, a towering demigod in pristine, magically-sealed armor, determined that both factions had broken the peace of the Zone. With terrifying, mechanical efficiency, the Knight obliterated the Liquidator forces. When Haniel’s panicked, uneducated parents blindly fired their crossbows and pistols at the newcomer, the Unifier cut them down without a second thought. 

Slumping over their dog, coughing up blood, Haniel looked up. Coming from the filth and regression of Dregsburg, this Knight didn't look like a soldier; They looked like a literal god. The dying child didn't see a monster who had just killed their parents; they saw a divine savior who had silenced the terrifying noise and erased the men trying to burn their home AND saved them from home. As the Knight turned and walked away, Haniel reached out a bloody hand, entirely enamored. They fell parasocially, obsessively in love with the absolute authority and "mercy" of the Unifiers, their heart stopping with an idolized image of Knights burned into their mind. 

(Present Day: 134 ZCE)

They were dead, their body long rotted away, but their spirit remained as a slightly glowing and translucent body. Today, Haniel roams the zone, removed of their dog, instead, doing everything they can do to maybe be anything like their "savior".
