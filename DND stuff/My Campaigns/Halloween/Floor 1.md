# Locations
## Encampments
Area where the new people reside. The reason why they're alive is because they're too scared to come out due to the weird gurgling sounds, which in turn was the sin of gluttony saving them.
## Elevator
Outside of the encampments, requires the elevator key to operate.
## IHOP
Key area. Where the sin of gluttony resides. Upon reaching the IHOP, the floor shifts and a mouth shifts over, swallowing the party into the glutton realm.
### Gluttonous realm
Here, the Acid storms ebb and flow in random intervals, causing the ecosystem to reset. A strange plague has taken root, which births mutations of animals which fight each other in the depths of the stomach to then rise up quickly, causing instant natural selection.
#### The Esophagus
A one way tunnel, the party falls down for quite a while. After a fall, the esophagus subtely levels out until it becomes a slide, where a tongue of a mysterious creature can be used as a raft, we go into initiative where someone has to become the raft controller and dodge obstacles and the others can help. 
1. A tooth is in the path.  Someone has to make a dex check to swerve it.
2. The tongue starts drooling. Make a strength check to stay on.
3. Acid waterfall in the way. Make a con check if you touch it.
4.  There is a toll area. You can either go through ez pass or normal toll. If you go through ez pass, it gets marked as you didn't pay and if you go through toll, you have to interact with a calderone who tries to get as much out of you that he can. 
#### True Stomach
It eventually drops the party in the tru stomach, which is an acid ocean dotted with meat lands, the one with the party has a porta potty with a talking toilet which has a **(something which lets you enter dreams)** you have to console it to have it open. Every 5 minutes, the oceans subside and maze of reef like formations are revealed. Instantly from the depths, a seething war of red and green can be seen frothing. Out of it comes various mutations of creatures which can fight but don't live long.

##### The People of the flesh
As they journey on ship, they meet a person wating for them, who introduces herself as Eldiah. She comes from the people of the flesh, a species of sentient beings who live only for a short time before going to old age and die. She claims to have been told in a dream by a person holding a scythe, that they would be coming and she should come help them. She opens a bag and its a bag of holding which looks like a mouth, but it holds perfectly fine IHOP menu items. She peacefully helps them leave out of the anus of gluttony.
